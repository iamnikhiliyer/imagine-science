# Revision 2 — a process carried by moving objects

The first draft repeated a title/illustration layout. Most of its movement was
entrances, fades, and a playhead. The review established legibility and technical
correctness but overrated motion quality. The user correctly identified it as
slide-like. The earlier 9/10 score is not an accepted quality judgment.

## New visual contract

One central animated schematic persists for the entire film. The camera moves
around it; source material, editable layers, and temporal frames connect to it.
The image changes because the narrated workflow changes. Use a dark, softly lit
workspace, restrained mint/ivory material detail, and dimensional glass planes.
Typography identifies actions briefly; the animation carries the explanation.

The neuron-like network is an original animated schematic, with no measured or
simulated experimental data. The film visualizes animation production, not a
biological fabrication process. Narration and aligned captions are unchanged.

| Time | Visible action |
| --- | --- |
| 0–3.6s | Start within a moving network, then pull back to reveal the film plane |
| 3.6–10.8s | Reference notes and the real narration waveform connect to that plane |
| 10.8–17.0s | Trace and cell layers separate in depth, then assemble again |
| 17.0–22.8s | The scene unfolds into temporal frames; a selected frame freezes, enlarges, and receives a visible label correction |
| 22.8–30.7s | The edited scene resumes moving, carries its corrected annotation, and joins the closing identity |

## Review criteria

Inspect continuous movement, causal order, camera continuity, and holds as well
as individual frames. A clear static frame is not sufficient evidence of a good
motion sequence. The selected-frame correction must be large enough to notice,
and that correction must persist after inspection. The opening close-up must
avoid partially cropped labels. The same protagonist must survive each phase.

The original presentation draft remains available as `Presentation-Draft` in
Studio's Earlier-Draft folder for comparison.

## Validation and actual coverage

Two visual passes: eight representative process frames, followed by 46 frames
including narration boundaries and authored motion extrema. Inspected a selected
16-frame overview and the refinement frame at full resolution. Corrected the
opening's cropped labels, enlarged the selected frame during correction, and
kept the corrected annotation on the resumed animation.

The Studio preview was exercised with live playback and browser sampling of the
camera transform and film-frame counter. Those checks establish runtime motion;
they do not substitute for audience judgment of pacing or quality. The existing
narration and synchronized captions were preserved. No every-frame viewing or
final MP4 export is claimed.

All 27 tests pass, including five new choreography tests for camera continuity,
the persistent actor, layer/inspection order, the inspection freeze, and resumed
movement. Manifest validation, lint, TypeScript, and the production bundle pass.
